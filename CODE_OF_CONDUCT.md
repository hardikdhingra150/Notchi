# Code of Conduct

Notchi follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

Report problems by opening a private security advisory or contacting a maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers will review every report and may remove, edit or reject contributions, or ban anyone who breaks these rules.
